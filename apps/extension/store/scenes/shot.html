A field guide to light roasts

Light roasts keep more of what the farm put into the bean. They are also the least forgiving roast to brew: a few degrees or a few seconds decide whether a cup tastes of apricot or of hay.

What “light” means

A roast is called light when it is dropped shortly after first crack. The bean has lost about 12% of its weight and no oil has reached the surface.

RoastDrop temperatureWeight loss
Light196 to 205 °C11 to 13%
Medium210 to 220 °C14 to 16%
Dark225 to 240 °C17 to 20%

Brewing one well

Grind finer than you would for a medium roast, and use water just off the boil.
